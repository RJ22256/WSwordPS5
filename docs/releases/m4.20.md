**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 20: 1080p scaled up to 1440p or 4K.**

**Video > Resolution** has two new choices:

- **1440p from 1080p:** the game is drawn at 1080p and AMD FSR scales it up to 1440p.
- **4K from 1080p:** the game is drawn at 1080p and AMD FSR scales it up to 4K.

Drawing at 1080p is lighter on the GPU than drawing at 1440p. That leaves more room for Ray-Tracing and keeps the frame rate steadier in heavy scenes. The picture is a little softer.

As with the other choices, a change applies the next time WSword starts: close it from the PS5's home screen and start it again.

Also included: everything in fix 19 (faster, steadier Ray-Tracing and the Resolution setting).
