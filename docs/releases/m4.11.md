**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 11: the HUD's button diamond matches a PlayStation controller.**

The action prompt ("Place", "Pick up", "Talk"...) draws a diamond of the four face buttons in the Switch's layout, with A on the right and B at the bottom. A is Cross on the PS5, so the diamond showed Cross on the right and Circle at the bottom, the opposite of the controller in your hands.

Cross and Circle now swap places in it, together with the prompt's label and the highlight under them. Cross sits at the bottom and Circle on the right, with Triangle on top and Square on the left, as on a DualSense. The buttons themselves don't change: Cross still does the action and Circle still cancels.

The log confirms it at startup: `[fs] HUD diamond: Cross at the bottom and Circle on the right, as on the controller`.

Also included: fix 10's frame generation fix and fix 9's dedicated cores.
