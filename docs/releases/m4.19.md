**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 19: faster, steadier Ray-Tracing and a Resolution setting.**

**Ray-Tracing (Quality mode)**
- **About half the GPU time for the rays.** Each frame now traces half as many rays, turned a little each frame and blended with the frames before. Over a few frames that adds up to as many as before. Heavy forest scenes should gain the most.
- **No more lines on far objects.** Past about 50 m the ray-traced shadows hand over smoothly to the game's own, which take over fully by 90 m. Far away, the shadow casters' shapes are coarse and drew lines across what they shadowed. The rays also stand a little further off surfaces at a distance.
- **Grass no longer shimmers.** Blending with the last frames evens out the flicker. On grass and leaf edges, the rays no longer shadow a blade by itself.
- Ambient occlusion stops at 60 m, where it was less than a pixel anyway.

**Video > Resolution**
- **Automatic** (as before): the TV's own. On a 4K TV the game is drawn at 1440p and AMD FSR scales it up.
- **1080p:** drawn at 1080p for a 1080p TV. This is the lightest on the GPU and leaves the most room for Ray-Tracing.
- **1440p:** drawn at 1440p and shown as it is (native).
- **4K:** drawn at 1440p, AMD FSR scales it up to 4K.

A size you choose comes before 120 Hz, at 120 Hz where the TV takes it at that size. **A change applies the next time WSword starts:** close it from the PS5's home screen and start it again.
