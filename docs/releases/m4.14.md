**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 14: a PlayStation-style controller on the Controls screen.**

The pause menu's System > Controls screen (the Basic Controls, horse and Sand Seal pages) used to show a drawing of the handheld Switch. It now shows a two-grip controller in the DualSense layout:

- the d-pad on the upper left and the face buttons (Triangle, Circle, Cross, Square) on the upper right
- both sticks lower down toward the middle
- a touch pad in the centre, with Create and Options beside it, and the PS and mute buttons below
- a top view with L1/L2 and R1/R2

The labels' lines were rerouted to the new button positions on all three pages. Where the order on the controller changed, the labels moved too. For example, on the horse and Sand Seal pages the d-pad labels now sit above the left-stick labels.

The drawing is original artwork in the style of the game's own picture. The log confirms it: `[fs] controls screen: lines led to the two-grip pad's buttons` at startup, and `[pad] controls drawing 0 ... 1 ...` when the screen is first opened.

Also included: fix 13's 4K support and the earlier HUD and frame-generation fixes.
