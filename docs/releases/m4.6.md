**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 6: the TV output at each mode's resolution, and the CPU and GPU in the overlay.**

- **Output per mode:** your log showed the game holding 60 fps in Performance but with little room to spare. It was drawn at 1080p, then the console's GPU scaled it to 4K, and the HUD and the final passes were also drawn at 4K, every frame. WSword now sends the TV the mode's own resolution and lets the TV scale it to its panel:
  - Performance outputs **1080p**.
  - Quality outputs **1440p** (your TV offers 2560x1440).

  The work the GPU did at 4K is gone. Quality is also sharper than before, because it's drawn at 1440p with no AMD FSR 1 scaling. If you switch modes in play, the new mode draws at its own resolution on the current output, and the output follows at the next start.
- **Performance overlay** (WSword menu > Display > Performance overlay: full):
  - **CPU:** WSword's own use of the 13 cores the console gives a game, and the CPU temperature.
  - **GPU:** how busy it is, timed on the GPU itself (the console has no driver figure to read), and the SoC temperature.
  - **Memory:** direct memory.

  Near 100% GPU means the graphics settings are the limit. A low GPU figure with a frame rate below target means the CPU is.
