## WSword PS5 0.3.29-m4

### amiibo: pictures in the bar

Each amiibo can now show its own picture in the amiibo bar, fitted in its slot like the game's item icons.

- **Getting them:** go to Controls → amiibo → **Pictures** and press it once. WSword downloads all 932 pictures to this device from AmiiboAPI's online amiibo database, four at a time.
  - It takes a few minutes and about 30 MB. The pictures are shrunk to fit the bar, at most 160 px a side.
  - The row shows the progress. The bar picks up new pictures as they arrive.
- **Missed downloads:** if some don't come through, **Download the missing pictures again** tries those once more.
- **Your own pictures:** put a PNG named after the figure's id in `amiibo/images/` (e.g. `01030000024f0902.png`) and the bar shows it the same way.
- **Removing them:** **Delete pictures** removes them all.
- **Without pictures:** the bar draws each figure in its series' colour, as before.

The pictures are Nintendo's product photos from AmiiboAPI's database. They're fetched to your own device only when you ask, and none of them ship with WSword.

The PS5 downloads them with the console's own HTTP library; it needs the console to be online.

Install as before: replace the PPSA99777 folder's files with this zip's.

Made without testing on the console.
