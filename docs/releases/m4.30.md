## WSword PS5 0.3.30-m4

### New: Slow Time, a WSword ability

**Press both sticks in together (L3 + R3)** and the whole world slows to a third of its speed for seven seconds: enemies, arrows, falling rocks, the time of day.

- **When it starts:** a ring of light sweeps out from the middle of the screen, the edges cool to a Sheikah blue, and a low *whoom* plays.
- **While it runs:** an hourglass with a draining arc at the top of the screen shows the time left.
- **When it ends:** the colours warm back with a rising chime.
- **Cooldown:** 20 seconds, then you can use it again.
- **Where it works:** never in the menus, cutscenes or while paused; the world runs normally there.
- **Link slows with the world.** It works like the game's own mid-air bow slow motion, but whenever you want: more time to react, line up a shot or watch a fight.
- **Turning it off:** Settings → Controls → **Abilities** → *Slow Time (L3 + R3)*. It's on by default.

It works with the game's own clock, which WSword already runs, so no part of the game's code is changed.

Install as before: replace the PPSA99777 folder's files with this zip's.

Made without testing on the console.
