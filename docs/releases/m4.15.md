**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 15: the DualSense's own features.**

- **Haptics:** the game's HD rumble now plays through the DualSense's haptic actuators instead of the old two-motor emulation. The game sets each Joy-Con's low and high band (strength and pitch) every frame, and WSword plays exactly that: the left Joy-Con's on the left side, the right's on the right. You'll feel more detail in things like footsteps, the horse, shield surfing and lightning. If the console refuses the haptic output, it falls back to the motors as before. The log says which: `[ps5] pad: haptics` or `two-motor rumble`.
- **Light bar:** it follows Link's hearts during play: green above half, amber below half, red below a quarter, and a red pulse at one heart or less. It turns PlayStation blue in menus, on the title screen and on loading screens. The log shows `[dualsense] hearts .../...` once it has found them.
- **Adaptive triggers:** R2 now feels like drawing a bow, with resistance through the pull and a click near the end. L2 has light, even resistance for focusing. Both triggers go slack in menus.
- **Touch pad:** pressing it still works as before, and in the pause menu you can now swipe left or right to change page.

Also included: the controls-screen picture support (fix 14) and 4K support (fix 13).
