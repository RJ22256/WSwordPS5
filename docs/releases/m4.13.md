**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 13: 4K TV support.**

On a 4K TV, WSword now outputs 4K. Both modes still draw the game at 1440p, and AMD FSR scales the picture up to 2160p on the console. The scaling happens on the PS5 rather than in the TV, which gives a sharper result.

- **4K TV:** 4K output, with the 1440p picture upscaled by FSR.
- **1440p TV:** 1440p as before, with nothing upscaled.
- **1080p TV:** the game draws at 1080p.
- **Frame generation and 120 Hz:** with frame generation on, 120 Hz is chosen first. A TV that takes 4K at 120 Hz gets 4K at 120. A TV that only takes 120 Hz at 1440p gets 1440p at 120, so you keep the smooth 120. The display mode is picked when WSword starts, so if you turn frame generation on or off, restart to change it.

The log shows what was picked: `[ps5] display ...: 3840x2160 at 119.88 Hz` and `[mode] ... drawn at 1440p, AMD FSR 1 up to the output`.

Also included: fix 12's round diamond icons, fix 11's controller-accurate diamond, fix 10's frame generation fix and fix 9's dedicated cores.
