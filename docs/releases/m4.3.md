**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 3: two modes, Performance and Quality.** On the console, the PC's four modes (Battery, Performance, Turbo, Unlocked) are replaced by two:

- **Performance** (the default): 60 fps at 1080p, with the game's own draw distance and sharper shadow maps than the Switch's.
- **Quality**: 30 fps, with Ray-Tracing (sun shadows, ambient occlusion and water reflections), Extreme+ draw distance and the sharpest shadow maps.

You switch between them in the WSword menu (touch pad + OPTIONS) > Display > Mode, or on the start screen. If you change any of those settings yourself, the mode shows as Custom.
