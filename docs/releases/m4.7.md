**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 7: 120 Hz, and frame generation from 60 to 120 in Performance.**

Your last log showed the console offering 119.88 Hz once your TV was on 120, but WSword picked 59.94. It now takes the 120 Hz mode whenever the TV offers it.

- **Performance with frame generation** (Lossless 2x or AMD FSR 3, in WSword menu > Display > Frame generation): the game runs at 60 and **120** frames a second reach the TV.
- **Performance without frame generation:** 60, each frame shown for exactly two of the TV's refreshes, evenly paced as a console game is.
- **Quality:** 30, or 60 with frame generation.
- **Shown frame rate:** the mode's frame rate on screen follows the output. On a 60 Hz output, Performance stays at 60 either way.

For 120 Hz, turn on the PS5's **Settings > Screen and Video > Video Output > 120 Hz Output (Automatic)** and use a TV and HDMI port that take 120 Hz. The log says what it got: `[mode] Performance on a 1080p 120 Hz output ... 120 fps on screen`.
