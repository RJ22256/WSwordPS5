**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 16: an HDR look.**

Real HDR output isn't available to a homebrew title on the PS5. WSword's picture can only reach the TV in standard range. As the next best thing, **Video > HDR look** (touch pad + OPTIONS) makes the picture feel like HDR on any TV:

- deeper blacks
- more contrast in the mid-tones
- brighter highlights
- richer colour, with the dullest colours boosted most

It's a colour grade of WSword's own (`colour-luts/HDR_Look.cube`), so it replaces any LUT chosen under Colour > LUT. If your TV has an SDR-to-HDR or "HDR effect" mode, turning that on as well adds to it.

Also included: the DualSense features (fix 15).
