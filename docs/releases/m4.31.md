## WSword PS5 0.3.31-m4

### A new start screen

The start screen now uses the same black look as the in-game quick menu, as on WSword for Android.

- **Top left:** WSword's icon, its name and your game version.
- **Top right:** your TV's refresh rate and resolution.
- **The sheet along the bottom:** your journey and when you last saved, then five tiles: **Play** (or **Continue**), **Mode** (Performance / Quality: press to switch), **Saves**, **Mods** and **Settings**. A white ring follows the selected tile.
- **Below the tiles:** **Set up game files** (only while they still need setting up) and **Quit**.

**Settings** from the start screen opens the same page as the in-game All settings: categories down the left (L1 / R1 to switch), options on the right, and each option's description along the bottom.
- The categories are Files, Video, Colour, Audio, Controls, Language, Mods and Saves.
- Circle goes back to the start screen.

### Ray-Tracing removed

Ray-Tracing is gone from the PS5 version. It's no longer an option in Video, the quick menu or the hotkeys.

- **Quality mode** is now 30 fps with High draw distance and the sharpest shadows, without Ray-Tracing.
- **The quick menu** has four tiles: Resume, Mode, Frame generation and Performance overlay.
- **If you had it on,** it stays off; your other settings stay as they were.

### The WSword Shirt

**Settings → Mods → WSword Shirt** turns the Nintendo Switch Shirt into the WSword shirt: navy, with the WSword emblem where the Switch logo was. This applies both on Link and on its inventory icon. Its name and description change in every language too.

- **Making it:** turning it on makes the shirt from your own game files on the console, in under a minute. The status line says when it's ready, and it applies the next time the game starts.
- **Turning it off:** the shirt becomes the Switch's again.
- **Your saves:** nothing in them depends on it.

Only WSword's emblem ships with the title, in `assets/wsword-emblem.png`. The shirt itself is made from your own game files.

### amiibo pictures

Two fixes from WSword for Android:
- **Blank pictures:** a few of AmiiboAPI's pictures are WebP files with a .png name and showed up blank. They now show.
- **Scrolling crash:** scrolling through all 900+ figures could run the menu out of picture slots and crash. The bar now keeps at most 40 pictures loaded and lets the oldest go.

Install as before: replace the PPSA99777 folder's files with this zip's.

Made without testing on the console.
