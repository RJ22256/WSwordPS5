**WSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 4: Quality at 1440p.**

- **Quality:** 30 fps, with the game drawn at **1440p**, Ray-Tracing, Extreme+ draw distance and the sharpest shadow maps.
- **Performance:** 60 fps, with the game drawn at **1080p**.
- **Output:** WSword now sends the TV its highest resolution, up to 4K. AMD FSR 3 scales each mode's picture up to that: ×1.5 from 1440p, ×2 from 1080p. Where FSR 3 can't run, FSR 1 does it instead.
- **On a 1080p output:** both modes are drawn at 1080p. Set the PS5's video output (Settings > Screen and Video > Video Output > Resolution) to Automatic or 2160p to get 4K.

The log lists every mode the TV offers (`[ps5] display mode`) and what each WSword mode draws at (`[mode]`).
