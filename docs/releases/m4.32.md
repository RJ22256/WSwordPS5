## WSword PS5 0.3.32-m4

Two fixes for 0.3.31-m4.

### Crash while loading, with frame generation on

With FSR 3 frame generation on, the game closed as the world finished loading, just as frame generation started. FSR 3 names its GPU work for debugging tools, and the converter it used for those names fails on the PS5 and closed the game. It now uses a converter that can't fail. Frame generation works as before. This had nothing to do with the amiibo pictures.

### The WSword Shirt couldn't be made

Turning on **Settings → Mods → WSword Shirt** said "the WSword emblem isn't in the title folder". The emblem was left out of 0.3.31-m4's zip. It now ships as `wsword-emblem.png`, beside eboot.bin. Copy this zip's files over the PPSA99777 folder, then turn the shirt on again.

### amiibo pictures on a console without the internet

The Pictures button downloads from AmiiboAPI, so it needs the console online. On a console that can't reach the internet, download the pictures on a PC and copy them to the console's amiibo pictures folder over FTP, as before.

Install as before: replace the PPSA99777 folder's files with this zip's.

Made without testing on the console.
